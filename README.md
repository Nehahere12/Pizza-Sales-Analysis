PIZZA SALES REPORT AND PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS | POWER BI & SQL <br>
<br>
PROJECT OVERVIEW <br>
This project features an interactive Pizza Sales Analysis dashboard created using Power BI and backed by SQL queries. It provides key operational and financial insights, including sales trends, category performance, peak ordering periods, and detailed performance rankings for top and bottom selling menu items. <br>
<br>
KEY FEATURES AND VISUALIZATIONS <br>
- Executive Summary KPIs: Tracks key sales metrics including Total Revenue ($817.9K), Total Orders (21,350), Total Pizzas Sold (49,574), Average Order Value ($38.31), and Average Pizzas Per Order (2.32). <br>
- Temporal Trend Analysis: Visualizes order trends across days of the week (peaking on Friday and Saturday evenings) and months of the year (peaking in July and January). <br>
- Sales Distribution Breakdown: Breaks down sales and revenue across pizza categories (Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie) and pizza sizes (Large, Medium, Small). <br>
- Best and Worst Sellers: Ranks Top 5 and Bottom 5 pizzas by total revenue, total quantity sold, and total orders to highlight key drivers and underperforming items. <br>
<br>
TECH STACK AND TOOLS USED <br>
- Power BI Desktop: Interactive visual reporting, dynamic navigation buttons, slicers, and DAX calculations. <br>
- SQL: Query formulation, KPI aggregation, and data validation. <br>
- Data Modeling: Efficient data structuring to evaluate temporal patterns and product performance metrics. <br>
<br>
KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS <br>
- Revenue Leaders: The Thai Chicken Pizza generates the highest revenue, while The Classic Deluxe Pizza leads in total orders and quantities sold. <br>
- Underperforming Items: The Brie Carre Pizza contributes the least to overall revenue, total quantity, and order volume. <br>
- Customer Preferences: Large size pizzas generate the maximum sales (45.89%), and the Classic pizza category accounts for the largest share of overall orders (30.03%). <br>
